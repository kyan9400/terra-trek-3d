How we route

Every journey starts with a map and a long conversation.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A 45-minute call with a route designer. We ask what you want to feel, not only where you want to go.

  2. 02

    Route

    We sketch three possible lines across the map, each with honest trade-offs: time, cost, flights and pace.

  3. 03

    Ground

    Every stop is run by people who live there. We pay guides above local rates and publish what they earn.

  4. 04

    Return

    Back home, you get a printed map of the trip you actually took — detours, wrong turns and all.

Questions

Good questions

How fit do I need to be?

If you can walk 10 km on a mixed path, you can do every route we run. Harder options (Lares, the Routeburn) always have a gentler alternative on the same day.

Can I start the route somewhere else?

Yes. The Long Way Round is a loop on paper; privately, we can start in any of the six cities and fly it in either direction.

What is included in the price?

Stays, guides, every ground and rail leg, most meals and carbon removal. Long-haul flights are quoted separately so you can use your own miles.

How do you handle flight emissions?

We measure each flight, then buy twice that amount in verified removal — not avoidance credits — and send you the certificates.

Can I travel solo?

Many of our travellers do. Expedition groups have no single supplement on shared-room dates.
